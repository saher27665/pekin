About Us

Our Journey from Sialkot, Pakistan to Beijing & Dubai

Who We Are

PEKIN-PAK-INTERNATIONAL LLC is a premier Import Export & General Traders Commission Agent with a rich legacy spanning over three decades.

As a trusted commission agent and general trader, we specialize in connecting global manufacturers with buyers across the world.

Our mission is to empower construction and mining projects with world-class machinery and unparalleled support across the globe.
