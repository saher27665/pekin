Who We Are

PEKIN-PAK-INTERNATIONAL LLC is a premier Import Export & General Traders Commission Agent with a rich legacy spanning over three decades. Our corporate headquarters is located in Sialkot, Pakistan — a city renowned globally for its excellence in manufacturing and export.

From this foundation of quality and craftsmanship, we have expanded our operations to Beijing, China (2007) and Dubai, UAE (2008), establishing a truly global presence.

As a trusted commission agent and general trader, we specialize in connecting global manufacturers with buyers across the world. Our core expertise lies in construction and mining machinery.

Our Journey

Established in Sialkot, Pakistan in 1993 with a vision to bridge the gap between manufacturers and global markets. Sialkot, known as the 'City of Export,' provided the perfect foundation for our operations.
