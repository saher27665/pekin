About Us

Our Journey from Sialkot, Pakistan to Beijing & Dubai

Who We Are

PEKIN-PAK-INTERNATIONAL LLC is a premier Import Export & General Traders Commission Agent with a rich legacy spanning over three decades. Our corporate headquarters is located in Sialkot, Pakistan — a city renowned globally for its excellence in manufacturing and export. From this foundation of quality and craftsmanship, we have expanded our operations to Beijing, China (2007) and Dubai, UAE (2008), establishing a truly global presence.

As a trusted commission agent and general trader, we specialize in connecting global manufacturers with buyers across the world. Our core expertise lies in construction and mining machinery, ensuring that our clients receive the highest quality equipment for their projects.

Our mission is to empower construction and mining projects with world-class machinery and unparalleled support across the globe.

Our Journey

PEKIN-PAK-INTERNATIONAL LLC was established in Sialkot, Pakistan in 1993 with a vision to bridge the gap between manufacturers and global markets.

In 2007, we took a significant step forward by establishing our Beijing Representative Office in the heart of China's capital.

The following year, in 2008, we extended our reach to Dubai, UAE, positioning ourselves at the crossroads of global trade and commerce.
