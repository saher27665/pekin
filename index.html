Our Story

PEKIN-PAK-INTERNATIONAL LLC was founded in 1993 in Doha, Qatar, as a premier import-export and general trading company. For over three decades, we have been the trusted bridge between global manufacturers and buyers across the Middle East, Asia, and Africa.

Our journey began with a simple promise: to source the best machinery at the best prices, with complete transparency and trust. Today, that promise remains at the heart of everything we do.

Our reputation is built on relationships, not transactions. Every machine we source carries our name — and our word.
