Our Story

Built on trust & expertise.

PEKIN-PAK-INTERNATIONAL LLC is a premier Import Export & General Traders Commission Agent with a legacy spanning over three decades. Our corporate headquarters is in Sialkot, Pakistan — a city renowned globally for excellence in manufacturing and export.

From this foundation of quality and craftsmanship, we expanded to Beijing, China (2007) and Dubai, UAE (2008), establishing a truly global presence.

As a trusted commission agent and general trader, we specialize in connecting global manufacturers with buyers across the world. Our core expertise lies in construction and mining machinery.
